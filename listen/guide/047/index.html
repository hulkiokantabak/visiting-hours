Forty-one. Nothing Worth Keeping

Listen

Synthetic narration

The bedtime landscape is an attempt to leave nothing chargeable. He keeps a road, a hedge, an empty field and an open gate. He excludes names, numbers, lemon and music. Once he included a green door and woke with a four-unit fee in his head. This caution belongs to him; the story does not establish that all unnamed stories are free.

Their shared stock is one hundred ninety-four retained words, surviving a second asking. The announced new-year threshold is three hundred. Subtracting their stock leaves a shortfall of one hundred six words. That is a calculation, not another charge. The words are counted between them, and the passage does not divide that total into two separate personal accounts.

He tells the road only as far as the gate. She supplies his turning over, his free sleep, and words he is about to say. Sleep-talk remains a possible explanation. Her account is unfinished, so it provides no complete forecast. When she says he lets go of her hand, he answers, Not yet. The ending is contested at the point of contact.
