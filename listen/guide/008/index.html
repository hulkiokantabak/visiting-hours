Six. Kept

Listen

Synthetic narration · Download audio

The diary changes under its reader's attention. When E. opens it before dawn, the diarist keeps the entry short. Later underlinings draw the writing toward particular details. An intimate memory receives a long entry. Missing pages are reported again, for the fourth time. At the final reading, E. underlines nothing, and another short entry follows. The diarist's gender is unstated, as are the identities of whoever controls the gaps or is being continued.

The calendar gives recurring Thursdays and the second week of June, but no reliable count of uninterrupted weeks. Two passages begin in the middle of a sentence. They make interruptions visible without proving two separately dated deletions. The report of a fourth disappearance tells us that pages are missing again, not what every lost page contained or how long the omissions span.

The service notice permits summaries and aggregate disclosure to the counterparty. Alongside that permission, the diary preserves bodily memory, E.'s underlinings and a morning deliberately left unphotographed. E.'s palm finally holds the cover shut, while the account of that act remains inside. Permission to summarize is not proof that any particular missing page was removed through that permission.
