TÜRKÇE

Two hills, no complete plan

Listen

Synthetic narration · Download audio

The city has a port and two hills. The near hill carries the funicular, its middle station, the dome and the last stop. Beyond that last stop the route keeps climbing, past a rope rail toward an open gate and the monastery. The connection runs from the hill upward; an exact remaining distance is withheld. The far hill has the works and cranes. Contracted weather belongs to this city, but rain also appears that nobody has paid for.

The pontoon supplies a reference for distances. It does not supply a complete map. The same water can be sea, or river or sea, while an Office calls it the channel. The fish market adds awnings, weather and a talking cat. These places are connected by stated routes and encounters. The guide does not assign fixed banks, coordinates or an exhaustive road network.
