TÜRKÇE

Fifty-nine. The Liminal

Listen

Synthetic narration

The house gives four phases of its stone voice. It understood for its first thousand years, then tired as wars and lovers returned. It let go, stopped counting steps, and allowed the city to happen. Now it clenches. Rain enters the mending and frost opens the wall there. An unseen form is expected to outlast it. These are narrated phases, not a fully dated history.

Two Office workers come to count visitors. One refuses food because they have work; his colleague, who climbed without water, removes his coat. The first writes him down as the first visitor and says the entry is only for the total. The colleague puts his coat back on and eats at the threshold while the house supports his back. No fee or later penalty is established.

Keeping takes several forms. A woman's spoon remains on a sill, unasked for. Biscuit takes a visitor's shoe, which an unnamed man returns to the door. The visitors' page is held unedited while its ink reaches one age, and the wall physically holds the worker's back. The dog is named, but the man is not identified as Rauf. Neither the spoon's owner nor the house's nature is resolved.
