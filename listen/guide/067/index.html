Final story. Visiting Hours

Listen

Synthetic narration

Beyond the funicular's last stop, she carries water, a return half-ticket and an unspoken reason. Older steps pass a rusted gate and warm wax in a niche. At the treeline her phone loses the city. Deliveries, traffic, harbor and channel fall out of hearing as she reaches the low white wall and open upper gate. This climb does not establish every law of the hill.

A visitor thanks her for water in a future summer while her bottle is still unopened. Two men stand between wars, one not yet happened. A man without faith sleeps on a prayer mat with covered feet. She recognizes a coin without explaining its history. Later she offers the smuggler the opened bottle, but his drinking is not shown. These encounters leave time, belief and identification unresolved.

In the evening she means to descend while there is light. The gate has remained open, with nobody using it downhill that day. Her return half-ticket has softened in her pocket. She goes back inside, then signs the visitors' book; the ink reaches the age of the other entries. Her duration remains open. The gate establishes neither imprisonment nor an irreversible decision to remain.
