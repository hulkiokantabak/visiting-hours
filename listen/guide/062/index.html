Fifty-five. The Well

Listen

Synthetic narration

The wishes range from a knee, money, a dog and a small yard to another Sunday with a voice or another first evening at the film. Some seek a new question or a breath returned to its place. Another prefers remaining for the hill instead of climbing it. Grouping these desires helps distinguish their scale without assigning identities or ranking who deserves fulfillment.

Several wishes echo earlier stories. The film evening still containing the boat recalls Four Minutes. Saturday's extended ceremony recalls The Standard Vows. A breath between accepted words recalls The Last Word, while the hour of open doors at three recalls Service Improvement. Such details invite recognition across the book, but they do not certify that each wisher is the protagonist of the earlier story.

The quarter-end procedure attends to material rather than requests. Coins are counted and returned to the general account. Paper is dried to establish whether it is money and then discarded. The wishes are never asked for or entered into the record. No preserved wish is described. Money leaving the well as an accounted sum does not show that anyone's wish was submitted or answered.
