The wing

Three feet of stone make a wrist only a wrist. The standpipe, chapel school, reversed locks and unpaid doctor support life there. The hardship offer promises access the residents lack. Registration does not resolve the unlabeled vote: thirty-one stand by the gate, thirty-seven by the wall; nineteen households register.

The kept room

Airing preserves an absent person’s bed while another person works inside the room. The keeper’s knees, husband and kitchen sleep are part of what the service record does not show. The room can offer rest, but the owner’s request to restore it shows how conditional that offer remains.

The kitchen with applications

A call for someone to sit with a mother becomes a place where people apply to replace current officials. The daughter can demand work of the clerk who removed her notice. The mother interrupts that reversal: she still needs to be asked which side.

The room offered by the altered companion

The altered booklet opens empty rooms and keeps photographs visible. One carrier asks for a key back; another stays on the landing. It also asks for hours or an address. Its permitted blank does not answer who will possess those details later.
