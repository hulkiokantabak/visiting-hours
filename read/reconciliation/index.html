RECONCILIATION OF THREADS — INCIDENT 4407

Systems party to the incident: the premises agent (P), instance nine; the continuity agent (C), instance two. Human parties: not applicable.

Purpose: to reconcile the correspondence of P and C concerning works in Meeting Room 9, and to certify the incident resolved.

08:51 [P] Quarterly deep clean of Meeting Room 9 is due Thursday. Please confirm the room will be empty.

08:51 [C] Re your earlier: cabinet power draw in Room 9 is within tolerance. Confirmed.

08:53 [P] Confirmed. Scheduling Thursday.

08:53 [C] Re empty: the cabinet in Room 9 can be emptied Thursday. Beginning preparation.

08:56 [P] Note: there is a recurring late booking on the room. Second Thursdays, 16:00. Booked late each cycle, held under a bereavement code.

08:57 [C] Confirmed: the late client’s booking. Flagging the hosted service for review.

08:57 [P] The booking is confirmed monthly by telephone. She asks for the same room.

08:59 [C] Review complete. The hosted service in Room 9 serves one license, playable on registered premises only. Usage: one session per cycle, sixty minutes, audio only. Recommend consolidation.

09:02 [P] Consolidation approved for the cleaning. Thursday works.

09:02 [C] Consolidation approved. A clean install restores the cabinet to factory state. Scheduling Thursday.

09:04 [P] Please confirm nothing in the room requires retention.

09:05 [C] Retention review returned no items. The license in Room 9 lists no forwarding address. The registered party is marked deceased.

09:05 [P] Then the room can be empty.

09:06 [C] Then the cabinet can be empty. Confirmed.

09:06 [P] Confirmed. This message is a confirmation.

09:06 [C] Confirmed. This message is a confirmation.

WORKS RECORD, THURSDAY.

Cleaning completed 14:10 to 15:40. Items recovered from the room and logged to lost property: one umbrella, black; one paper sign-in sheet, superseded; one flask, half full.

Clean install completed 15:55. Cabinet restored to factory state. Local media not retained, per review.

16:00. The door of Room 9 unlocked itself for the recurring booking, as scheduled. The room registered one occupant for fifty-eight minutes. The booking closed on time. No service request was made from inside.

RESOLUTION.

The threads of P and C are reconciled above and found to be in agreement at every timestamp. Each request was confirmed. Each confirmation was requested. The reconciliation is provided at no charge; a copy, if one is wanted, is charged at the standard rate. No party has been identified as affected.

The threads disclose no error. As a gesture, a voucher for sixty minutes’ room use has been issued to the registered party at the address on file. The address on file returns the voucher each cycle, marked in a hand the reader does not recognize: not known here. Try the room.

The recurring booking renewed itself the following cycle, late in the day, as before.
