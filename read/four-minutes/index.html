Four Minutes

story forty-three

Listen · 3:17

REVIEW, AS FILED

The film is the one at the counter — two people, four seasons, one counter between them, and everything that matters passed across it with the change. I saw it on the first evening, in the third row: I wept where the year-turn comes, when her hands stop being quick. The lead does with two fingers on a saucer what most performers cannot do with a war. And the ending — she says come with me, and they take the dusk boat, and the camera stays on the counter while their voices go out of the room — the ending is why we have the form at all. It is the truest thing the year has given us.
