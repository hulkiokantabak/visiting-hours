Order Was Restored

story twenty-six

Listen · 4:52

There are eleven of them on the roster and they are not called anything. The badge says a department that does not describe the work. In the halls they are the men and women in the good gray coats who arrive at a walking pace, and the walking pace is the training: the moment you run, the room knows something is wrong, and the room is what you are there for. Not the man. The room.

The scale has five steps and he has known them since he was twenty-three.

One is presence. You stand where you can be seen and the trouble usually returns to its seat. Two is the hand at the elbow, offered from the side, never the front. Three is the chair — you bring a chair, and a chair is very hard to shout in. Four is the quiet room, which is at the back of every hall in the city, and which has water, and two soft seats, and no clock, and no charge for the first twenty minutes. Five is not his to describe on paper. Five has its own form and the form is not filed at the hall. Twice this year a coat has come back from cleaning with the hall’s docket still in the pocket, and both times it was hook nine’s, and hook nine has not been asked about it, and does not offer.

The quiet room works. He has never said so out loud.

Today is a ramp. The board says two hundred and forty and a man at the front of the queue has two hundred and thirty-nine, and the clerk has said the thing the clerk must say, and the man has begun to explain about his mother.

He goes in at one. Presence. The man keeps talking, faster, the way they do when there is arithmetic in the way. Two: the hand at the elbow. The man’s arm is thinner than the coat suggested and does not resist, which is how he knows there will be no five today. Three, the chair, brought from the wall in one motion, and the man sits down into it as if the chair had been the thing he came for. The training calls this the collapse and does not say it unkindly; there is a paragraph on it, and the paragraph is accurate, and he has watched the accuracy work on nine hundred people.

At the quiet room the man says: “One word.” He has a flask with him, the half-size kind the market sells, and he holds it the whole time and does not open it.

“I know.”

“I taught her the long ones. I thought the long ones.”

He gets him water. On the board behind them, in the standard type, it says that words taught are not always words kept. He does not read it out. Twenty minutes is free. The man uses eleven of them, and stops, and asks whether he can come back on Thursday, and is told yes, and is told, correctly, that the rate rises in the new year and that Thursday is inside the year, and the man writes Thursday on his hand as though he might lose it.

The report goes in at four. There is one field for the intervention and the field takes a number, and the number is three, and there is a line beneath the field with room for eleven words, and he writes the same eleven words he always writes: Attended, chair, quiet room. No mark. Order was restored.

Nobody has ever asked him what the room was like before it was restored.

His own file is in arrears. Ninety-one, and a wife who is at two hundred and six with four years of Thursdays left in her, and he has run the numbers on a Sunday with the good pen and knows exactly what will happen to him if he ever stands at the front of a queue and begins to explain. He knows the walking pace that will come towards him. He knows the chair will be brought from the wall in one motion. He has trained six of the eleven himself, and two of them are very good, and one of them is better than he was.

At the end of the shift the coats go on the rail in the room behind the hall, eleven hooks, and the hooks are numbered, and his is four. Somebody has drawn a chair on the wall above hook one, badly, years ago, and every one of them has laughed at it at least once, and nobody has painted over it. On the way out he checks the quiet room the way you check a stove. Water in the jug. Two seats. No clock.

He puts the chair back against the wall himself, where it lives, where it can be got to in one motion by whoever is on tomorrow. On the stairs he thinks about the fish he did not buy on Tuesday, and whether it will still be there Thursday, and it will not, and that is the whole of the thought. It is not his shift tomorrow. He straightens it anyway, twice.
