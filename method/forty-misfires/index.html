Vitrine V

Forty misfires

A project that hides its instrument failures has an instrument problem it cannot see. Every misfire in this project is numbered in one file, with what it claimed, what was true, and what believing it would have cost.

The criterion

Published in the register itself, so the count can be recounted by another hand:

a misfire is a defect in a thing that MEASURES MECHANICALLY — a script, a regex, a limb, a parser, a matcher, a converter, a harness, a band, or a written method that governs one — and it is filed when the instrument returned a verdict, or a silence, the artefact does not support.

V-67

The check — the hard-rule scanner, CAUSAL_MORPH

It reported — 27 hypotheses, the standing report of the limb guarding the book’s oldest law

BLIND TO BACK-VOWEL TURKISH. The ablative hard-coded the FRONT-vowel «nden», so every «…dığından / …duğundan» was unmatchable at every run since the limb was armed — 5 sites in 4 files. Confirming it, the chair found a SECOND vowel hole in the same list: the future participle hard-coded the BACK-vowel «acağı», so «…eceği için» was unmatchable too (census 0)

the truth, docs/VERIFIER-LOG.md

five sites of the book’s most absolute rule outside the scan for the whole stage. All nine newly-visible sites were then censused and ALL NINE ACQUIT — the ablatives are comparatives or the complement of «emin ol-». The scan reports 27 → 36 and the tree stays GREEN: the instrument was blind and what it was blind to was innocent, which nobody could have known, and which is the whole reason a blind limb is not a clean one

V-71

The check — map_rows(), which hands each verifier the rows for its piece

It reported — the EPILOGUE’s rows

it matched on did[3:] — the slot digits of a DS-### id, but the string LOGUE for BOTH PROLOGUE AND EPILOGUE. LOGUE: matched every PROLOGUE: home, so the two forms were reading each other’s rows: four of the six the EPILOGUE’s brief printed named PROLOGUE, and the map names EPILOGUE nowhere at all

the truth, docs/VERIFIER-LOG.md

the book’s last page verified against another page’s rows, at the one element after which nothing may be printed in any edition. Found by the EPILOGUE’s own verifier, again by reading the SLICING and not the output. The numeric shorthand is now used only where the id is numeric

V-79

The check — ast.parse, verifying the checker after a patch

It reported — SYNTAX OK

the file was zero bytes. open(p,'w') had truncated the checker before the expression that raised, and an empty file parses

the truth, docs/VERIFIER-LOG.md

the instrument the whole stage measures with, emptied and then certified sound. Restored from the last commit; the patch now builds and validates the whole string BEFORE any file is opened, writes to a temp and os.replaces. The day’s law a third time, and the third victim was the verifier of the fix (catch C-101)

The two the register names are the two the classification exists for: a limb blind to whether anything had been read at all, and a limb that could only see a thing being added and never a thing being taken away.

the two that were not are the two that matter: the verification-status marker (V-64), blind to whether anything had been read at all, and the ink it exposed carried six blockers at Part V; and register C-24’s one-way limb (V-76), blind to anthropomorphism removed, where the audit that finally looked found eight subtractions with five of them in one shape.

The standing lesson

AND THIS IS THE STAGE’S OWN STANDING LESSON, filed here because the misfire ledger is where it belongs: NOT ONE of the blind limbs was found by reading the corpus. Every one was found by reading the PATTERN — the regex, the slice, the open call, the vowel table — because a limb that cannot fire is indistinguishable from a limb with nothing to report, and the corpus is exactly where that difference is invisible.

The number a register may not get wrong

Five counts of this stage’s misfires stand in the tree: four, five, six, seven, and the forty above. The first four are not wrong. They are answers to smaller questions, each honest at its own sitting — one counts only the project’s own record faults and stops in the second week of August, two others count a single week’s instrument repairs.

Not one of the first four carries a criterion. The number a register may not get wrong is its own, and the way it gets it wrong is by never saying what it counts.
