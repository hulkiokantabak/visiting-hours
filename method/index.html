Method

The English manuscript reached Revision 30 under the author’s direction. The Turkish edition was translated against that completed text, using the earlier translation where it still served the revised stories.

Checks considered each story’s narrator, time frame, cadence and physical details in context. The author’s corrections and suggestions from several language models were compared with the English source; suggestions were accepted or declined individually.

The old edition’s counts and rules do not govern this one. Model reviews are not independent human readings or a claim that the author reread and approved every final passage.
