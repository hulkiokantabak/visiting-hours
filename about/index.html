About

Visiting Hours is a collection of sixty works in five parts by Hulki Okan Tabak. This site presents the final English Revision 30 and its Turkish translation, Ziyaret Saatleri.

The earlier work developed with Claude; English revision and the current Turkish translation continued with Codex under the author’s direction.
