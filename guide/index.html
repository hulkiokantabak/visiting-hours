CITY GUIDE

with Diagrams to VISITING HOURS

A hundred and eighty diagrams to sixty stories · the maps, the persons, the places, and the city’s own rules and rates

THE COMPANION IS NOT THE BOOK.It measures the collection. It does not summarize it, and it does not resolve it.

Landscape edition, for screen and for the web. The diagrams are set upright and full width; a portrait edition, with the diagrams redrawn for that page, is issued alongside it.

A second edition of this guide exists; its figures were drawn separately, not rotated, and where the two differ the story is the authority.

The guide is in English. There is no Turkish edition of it.
