After Stories 51-59 and Visiting Hours

The remainder is not a solution

An act changes when it returns

Biscuit's taught game endangers a rescue; later a man returns the stolen shoe. Suna takes blood out of the Pool and brings it back, then holds a donor who asks her to let go. The paid breath moves in the public announcement. These returns alter the act's pressure rather than simply complete a pattern.

Related stories: The Observance, The Pool, The Last Word, The Liminal

Toward the open gate

The Well receives practical and impossible wishes without taking testimony. The Naming encounters people who already have names and people it cannot keep. The cat’s sayings travel by mouth. One visitor counts a route and comes down; the final visitor goes back inside and signs. Her duration, their relation and the gate remain open.
