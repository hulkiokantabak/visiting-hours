The forms acquire hands

The leaflet separates the person wanted from the person who will pay. An examiner keeps a coin longer than promised. A secretary takes struck ink on a wet cuff. In the corrections chat, adding an injured man exposes his home address. The form's effect happens through someone's action, not merely its vocabulary.
