A diagram is also a reading

What the lines do, and where they stop

Amounts

A bar compares quantities only when the unit is shared. A fee for recording two spoken words is not two retained words toward a crossing. A count of households is not a count of voters. Calculated gaps are labeled as calculations; they are not additional figures spoken by a character.

Related stories: The Wing, The Last Word

Arrows and alignments

A sequence follows an action or its telling. A network shows a stated relation, not an unseen command structure. Dashed lines mark reported, incomplete, disputed or interpretive connections. Parallel columns do not appoint a final account. Node letters are diagram labels. Color is emphasis only: it marks no truth, authority, faction, danger or canonical status.

A divided authority

The city has departments, employees, competing instructions and failures. A statement issued by an Office, printed in an invitation or voiced by an altered companion belongs to that source. It is not automatically a law governing every scene. A diagram can show a claim and its consequence without making the claim true.

Related stories: The Works, A Companion for New Arrivals, For Corrections Only, The Column, Service Improvement, Not Given

Differences kept in view

The loan slip can reverse the lender and borrower. The hotel remark can be remembered at a door and in a bed. A refused appointment can become a requested one on a receipt. These differences remain beside the scenes that produce them. Resolving them would alter the book this guide accompanies.
